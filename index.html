Music Glasses Mark 1 — music and calls in your own tortoiseshell frames

Rounded-square tortoiseshell acetate frames that look completely normal from the front. Two slim gold hinge pods sit 17 mm back on the wire temples, each holding a MAX98357A I²S amp, a 15 × 11 mm micro-speaker on the inner face and three flush touch inlays. Two tortoise ear pods ride on the acetate ear tips, under the hair: the right one carries an Adafruit QT Py ESP32 Pico (classic Bluetooth A2DP + HFP + BLE), the USB-C charger, the power switch and an SPH0645 I²S mic at its front edge, aimed down the jaw line; the left one carries a 120 mAh cell. A Flutter app talks to the glasses over BLE and helps you find them.

1 · MusicBluetooth musicA2DP sink "Muse Glasses" + AVRCP: stereo from your phone, one amp + speaker per side.
2 · CallsPhone callsHands-Free Profile (HFP 1.7 client): CVSD 8 kHz or mSBC 16 kHz wideband, mic in the right ear pod.
3 · TouchTap · long-press · swipeTap = play/pause or answer/hang up. Long-press = reject. Swipe = volume (call volume in a call).
4 · AppCompanion appFlutter + flutter_blue_plus: now playing, call state, volume, EQ, battery, settings.
5 · FindFind my glassesHotter/colder RSSI meter, where your phone was at the last disconnect, and a Play-sound chime that ignores mute.
6 · DiscreetInvisible from the frontNothing on the frame front; the cross-frame wire hides behind the top rim; the electronics hide under the hair.
Hobby prototype. Estimates: ~1 hour of music or ~45–60 min of calls from the 120 mAh cell (~3× with the 350 mAh option). Open-ear sound leaks a little. The mic sits ~10 cm from the mouth (behind the ear, for looks), so callers hear you a bit quieter and with more room sound than on earbuds. The firmware compiles but has not been run on hardware yet.

Inconspicuous by design

The wearer's hair covers the temples and ears, so the layout pushes everything that can move back there:

  • Frame front: nothing electronic, no wires, no LEDs. The cross-frame ribbon runs on the back face of the top rim, under a tortoise cover strip. Cleaner (but irreversible) option: route a 1 mm groove along the inside top edge of the rim and fill it with tinted UV resin.
  • Hinge pods: as slim (6.2 mm) and as far back (17 mm behind the hinge) as the amp breakout allows; outer face flush with the end piece; the head-side lid is tortoise. Speakers fire from the inner face; no grille on show. Gold paint, like the wire temples.
  • Behind-ear pods: QT Py, charger, switch, USB-C, battery and the mic. Tortoise, like the ear tips. Hidden by hair.
  • What still can't move back: the amps + speakers (they must sit near the ear canal, and moving the amps would add 4 more wires per side down the temple) and the touch pads (where a finger naturally lands).

The hinge pods are taller than the wire temple by design: the amp breakout (19 × 18 mm) sets their height, but from the front they stay inside the end piece's outline. Use a non-conductive finish over the touch pads.

  1. Measure your frames. Wire temple diameter (~1.5–1.8 mm), hinge-to-ear-tip distance, ear-tip thickness. Adjust the pod channels before printing.
  2. Print. Resin or PLA at 0.12 mm layers. Sand, prime, dry-fit on your head: pods must clear your temples, ear pods sit behind the ears. Look at it face-on in a mirror: nothing should show past the end pieces.
  3. Pin the hinges. Wires crossing a folding hinge fatigue fast. For v1 pin the hinges open (thread-lock the hinge screws) or leave a soft silicone-wire loop.

6 Touch inlays & gestures

Three copper-tape electrodes per hinge pod, inlaid flush in a 0.1 mm recess on the outer face and painted over. The ESP32 measures each pad's capacitance with touchRead().

  1. Cut & place. Three ~11 mm pads with 1.5 mm gaps; a chevron edge lets a finger overlap two pads mid-swipe.
  2. Solder the tabs on the inside of the pod wall, then seal the slot with UV resin.
  3. Paint. Non-conductive finish only (mica pigments are fine; aluminium-flake metallics are not — check with a meter).
  4. Tune. Firmware triggers at −15 % and releases at −10 % of a 64-sample baseline, with a 150 ms lockout. After painting, recalibrate from the app (Config bit 1).

7 Audio & Bluetooth stack

The original ESP32 (in the QT Py ESP32 Pico) runs classic Bluetooth (A2DP + AVRCP + HFP) and BLE on one chip. Music flows as A2DP (SBC) → 3-band EQ → I²S → 2 × MAX98357A. Each amp picks its channel from the stereo frame by its SD voltage.

  • Core: Arduino-ESP32 3.3.x · board "Adafruit QT Py ESP32" · default 3 MB app partition (8 MB flash).
  • Libraries: ESP32-A2DP 1.8.10+ and arduino-audio-tools. BLE on the core's Bluedroid stack — NimBLE is reported to crash alongside A2DP.
  • Mode: BTDM (dual mode). No Wi-Fi, so no OTA: flash over USB.
  • RAM is tight: keep one custom GATT service, watch the free heap printed on connect and on every call, switch to BluetoothA2DPSinkQueued only if you hear dropouts.

8 Phone calls (HFP) & the mic

Route: plain Arduino, no ESP-IDF rebuild

I checked the prebuilt libraries that ship with Arduino-ESP32 3.3.12 (ESP-IDF 5.5.5): its sdkconfig.h for the ESP32 already sets CONFIG_BT_HFP_ENABLE, CONFIG_BT_HFP_CLIENT_ENABLE, CONFIG_BT_HFP_AUDIO_DATA_PATH_HCI, CONFIG_BT_HFP_WBS_ENABLE, CONFIG_BTDM_CTRL_BR_EDR_SCO_DATA_PATH_HCI and leaves CONFIG_BT_HFP_USE_EXTERNAL_CODEC off, so Bluedroid's own CVSD/mSBC codec is in. The pschatzmann ESP32-A2DP library only covers A2DP + AVRCP, so the sketch calls the raw esp_hf_client_* API next to it on the same Bluedroid instance. No ESP-IDF project or custom sdkconfig is needed.

  • Init: after a2dp.start() → set Class of Device (Audio, hands-free, telephony) → esp_hf_client_register_callback → esp_hf_client_init → esp_hf_client_register_data_callback(in, out).
  • Audio path: SCO over HCI. With the internal codec the stack hands the app 16-bit mono PCM (8 kHz CVSD or 16 kHz mSBC) in the incoming callback and pulls mic PCM through the outgoing callback after esp_hf_client_outgoing_data_ready(). These are ESP-IDF's "legacy" data callbacks (esp_hf_client_legacy_api.h): still present and linked in 5.5, but Espressif says the internal codec will be removed in a later IDF. Then switch to esp_hf_client_register_audio_data_callback + your own CVSD/mSBC codec.
  • Call control: CIND call / call-setup events → state (idle, incoming, outgoing, active), notified over GATT. Tap → esp_hf_client_answer_call() or hang up (esp_hf_client_reject_call() = AT+CHUP), long-press → reject, swipe → esp_hf_client_volume_update(SPK, 0–15). Phone-side volume changes (+VGS) update the local level.
  • I²S switching: music = TX only, 44.1 kHz/16-bit. When SCO comes up the firmware reopens I²S0 full duplex at 32 kHz/32-bit (the SPH0645 needs a 2.048–4.096 MHz bit clock), upsamples the far end ×2 (mSBC) or ×4 (CVSD) for the speakers and averages the mic down to 16/8 kHz. It switches back when SCO drops. The phone pauses A2DP during a call.
  • Echo: open-ear speakers ~3 cm from the ear and a mic ~6 cm behind them will leak. The phone's own AEC/NR stays on (the glasses do not send AT+NREC=0); the firmware adds half-duplex ducking (mic −12 dB for 150 ms whenever the far end is louder than a threshold) and keeps the call volume moderate. No on-chip AEC: ESP-SR/AEC doesn't fit next to A2DP + BLE on this chip.
  • RAM: 46.9 KB static (14 %), plus 2 × 4 KB ring buffers and a 4 KB audio task at runtime; Bluedroid HFP itself takes ~15–20 KB of heap when connected (estimate). The free heap prints on HFP connect and every SCO start: keep it above ~30 KB.

The mic: behind the ear, for looks

Adafruit I2S MEMS Microphone Breakout, SPH0645LM4H (#3421, $6.95) glued to the lower-front wall of the right ear pod, its bottom port over a 1 mm hole with a short printed horn, aimed forward-down along the jaw line. Wiring (all local): BCLK → A0, LRCL → A1 (shared with the amps), DOUT → GPIO22 (SDA1 on the STEMMA QT connector, not a strapping pin), SEL → GND, 3V, GND.

Trade-off: ~9–11 cm from the mouth instead of ~3 cm on a boom means ~10 dB less voice level and more room sound. Mitigations in this build: a sensitive MEMS mic (−26 dBFS @ 94 dB SPL), 6× digital gain (MIC_GAIN), the directional horn, foam sealing so it hears outside not inside the pod, and the phone's noise reduction. If callers still say you're far away, the next step is a second mic for beamforming. That costs RAM and another wire, so it's left out of v1.

9 Battery & charging

Protected 120 mAh 401230 cell in the left ear pod → its + lead crosses the frame (behind the top rim) to the slide switch in the right ear pod → switch OUT → QT Py VBAT+ (diode-protected, 3–6 V) and both amps' VIN. The Micro-Lipo charger (100 mA default, right for 120 mAh) sits on the cell side of the switch and is fed from the QT Py's 5 V pad, so the one USB-C port flashes and charges.

  1. Check polarity on the cell leads before soldering; insulate each joint immediately.
  2. Battery sense. 100 k / 100 k divider from switched VBAT to A3 (GPIO15, ADC2, strapping pin). Firmware maps 3.3–4.2 V to % every 5 s.
  3. Run time (estimates). Music ~100–130 mA → ~1 h. Calls ~110–150 mA (SCO keeps the radio busier, plus the mic and 32 kHz I²S) → ~45–60 min of talk. Connected idle ~25–40 mA → ~3–4 h of standby. 350 mAh ≈ 3×.
  4. Safety. Don't charge while wearing; keep the cell from being crushed in the ear pod; never use an unprotected cell.

10 Firmware (Arduino)

One sketch: A2DP sink + AVRCP callbacks, HFP hands-free client with call audio and the I²S mic, touch gesture machine (tap / long-press / swipe), BLE GATT server (incl. Find chime + Call state), battery monitor and EQ presets (saved in NVS). It compiles with arduino-cli for esp32:esp32:adafruit_qtpy_esp32_pico on core 3.3.12 with ESP32-A2DP 1.8.11 + arduino-audio-tools: 1,225,668 B flash (36 % of the 3 MB app slot), 46,928 B static RAM (14 %). It has not been run on hardware.

    if (v.length() < 1) return;
    uint8_t b = (uint8_t)v[0];
    if (c == chVol) setVolume(b);
    else if (c == chPlay) { if (b) a2dp.play(); else a2dp.pause(); }
    else if (c == chEq) { applyEq(b); notifyU8(chEq, gEq); }
    else if (c == chFind) { gChimeUntil = b ? millis() + 1000UL * b : 0; }
    else if (c == chCfg) { gInvertSwipe = b & 0x01; prefs.putBool("inv", gInvertSwipe); if (b & 0x02) gRecalibrate = true; }
  }
};

BLECharacteristic *mkChar(BLEService *s, const char *uuid, uint32_t props) {
  BLECharacteristic *c = s->createCharacteristic(uuid, props);
  if (props & BLECharacteristic::PROPERTY_NOTIFY) c->addDescriptor(new BLE2902());
  if (props & BLECharacteristic::PROPERTY_WRITE) c->setCallbacks(new WriteCB());
  return c;
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- touch
struct Pad { float base; bool down; uint32_t t0; };
Pad pads[6];

void calibrate() {
  for (int i = 0; i < 6; i++) {
    uint32_t sum = 0;
    for (int k = 0; k < BASELINE_N; k++) { sum += touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[i]); delay(2); }
    pads[i] = { sum / (float)BASELINE_N, false, 0 };
  }
}

struct Pod {            // gesture tracker per hinge pod
  int first = -1, last = -1; uint32_t tStart = 0, lockUntil = 0; bool active = false;
};
Pod pod[2];             // 0 = right, 1 = left

void onTap(int side) {                   // either pod
  if (gCall == CALL_INCOMING) { esp_hf_client_answer_call(); return; }
  if (gCall == CALL_ACTIVE || gCall == CALL_OUTGOING) { esp_hf_client_reject_call(); return; }   // AT+CHUP = hang up
  if (gPlaying) a2dp.pause(); else a2dp.play();                                                  // music: play / pause
}
void onLongPress(int side) {             // only meaningful while ringing
  if (gCall == CALL_INCOMING) esp_hf_client_reject_call();
}
void onSwipe(int side, int dir) {        // dir +1 = rear -> front (toward the lens)
  if (gInvertSwipe) dir = -dir;
  if (gCall != CALL_IDLE) {               // call volume, synced to the phone over HFP (+VGS)
    gCallVol = constrain((int)gCallVol + dir, 0, 15);
    esp_hf_client_volume_update(ESP_HF_VOLUME_CONTROL_TARGET_SPK, gCallVol);
    return;
  }
  setVolume(gVolume + dir * VOL_STEP);
}

void pollTouch() {
  const uint32_t now = millis();
  for (int s = 0; s < 2; s++) {
    Pod &p = pod[s];
    int touched = -1;
    for (int k = 0; k < 3; k++) {
      Pad &d = pads[s * 3 + k];
      float v = touchRead(TOUCH_PINS[s * 3 + k]);
      if (!d.down && v < d.base * (1.0f - TOUCH_TRIGGER)) { d.down = true; d.t0 = now; }
      else if (d.down && v > d.base * (1.0f - TOUCH_RELEASE)) d.down = false;
      if (!d.down) d.base += (v - d.base) / BASELINE_N;          // track drift only while untouched
      if (d.down && touched < 0) touched = k;
    }
    if (now < p.lockUntil) continue;
    if (touched >= 0) {
      if (!p.active) { p.active = true; p.first = p.last = touched; p.tStart = now; }
      else p.last = touched;
    } else if (p.active) {                                       // finger lifted: classify
      p.active = false;
      uint32_t dur = now - p.tStart;
      if (p.first != p.last && dur <= SWIPE_MAX_MS) { onSwipe(s, p.first > p.last ? +1 : -1); p.lockUntil = now + LOCKOUT_MS; }
      else if (dur >= TAP_MIN_MS && dur <= TAP_MAX_MS) { onTap(s); p.lockUntil = now + LOCKOUT_MS; }
      else if (p.first == p.last && dur >= LONG_PRESS_MS) { onLongPress(s); p.lockUntil = now + LOCKOUT_MS; }
    }
  }
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- HFP hands-free client (calls)
void onHfp(esp_hf_client_cb_event_t ev, esp_hf_client_cb_param_t *p) {
  switch (ev) {
    case ESP_HF_CLIENT_AUDIO_STATE_EVT:
      gScoUp = p->audio_stat.state == ESP_HF_CLIENT_AUDIO_STATE_CONNECTED || p->audio_stat.state == ESP_HF_CLIENT_AUDIO_STATE_CONNECTED_MSBC;
      gScoWide = p->audio_stat.state == ESP_HF_CLIENT_AUDIO_STATE_CONNECTED_MSBC;   // mSBC = 16 kHz, CVSD = 8 kHz
      gScoChanged = true;
      break;
    case ESP_HF_CLIENT_CIND_CALL_SETUP_EVT:
      if (p->call_setup.status == ESP_HF_CALL_SETUP_STATUS_INCOMING) gCall = CALL_INCOMING;
      else if (p->call_setup.status != ESP_HF_CALL_SETUP_STATUS_IDLE) gCall = CALL_OUTGOING;
      else if (gCall != CALL_ACTIVE) gCall = CALL_IDLE;
      gCallDirty = true; break;
    case ESP_HF_CLIENT_CIND_CALL_EVT:
      gCall = p->call.status == ESP_HF_CALL_STATUS_CALL_IN_PROGRESS ? CALL_ACTIVE : CALL_IDLE;
      gCallDirty = true; break;
    case ESP_HF_CLIENT_VOLUME_CONTROL_EVT:
      if (p->volume_control.type == ESP_HF_VOLUME_CONTROL_TARGET_SPK) gCallVol = p->volume_control.volume;
      break;
    case ESP_HF_CLIENT_CONNECTION_STATE_EVT:
      Serial.printf("HFP state %d, free heap %u\n", p->conn_stat.state, ESP.getFreeHeap());
      break;
    default: break;
  }
}
// Bluedroid's internal CVSD/mSBC codec hands us 16-bit mono PCM (8 or 16 kHz)
void hfpIncoming(const uint8_t *buf, uint32_t len) { if (rxRing) xRingbufferSend(rxRing, buf, len, 0); }
uint32_t hfpOutgoing(uint8_t *buf, uint32_t len) {
  if (!txRing) return 0;
  size_t got = 0; uint8_t *d = (uint8_t *)xRingbufferReceiveUpTo(txRing, &got, 0, len);
  if (!d) return 0;
  memcpy(buf, d, got); vRingbufferReturnItem(txRing, d);
  return got;
}
void hfpBegin() {
  esp_bt_cod_t cod = {};                                   // advertise as an audio hands-free device
  cod.major = ESP_BT_COD_MAJOR_DEV_AV; cod.minor = 0x02;
  cod.service = ESP_BT_COD_SRVC_AUDIO | ESP_BT_COD_SRVC_RENDERING | ESP_BT_COD_SRVC_TELEPHONY;
  esp_bt_gap_set_cod(cod, ESP_BT_INIT_COD);
  rxRing = xRingbufferCreate(4096, RINGBUF_TYPE_BYTEBUF);
  txRing = xRingbufferCreate(4096, RINGBUF_TYPE_BYTEBUF);
  esp_hf_client_register_callback(onHfp);
  esp_hf_client_init();
  esp_hf_client_register_data_callback(hfpIncoming, hfpOutgoing);
}

// I2S mode switch: music = TX 44.1 kHz 16-bit (A2DP -> EQ); call = full duplex 32 kHz 32-bit (mic needs 64 BCLK/frame)
void i2sMusicMode() {
  i2s.end();
  auto c = i2s.defaultConfig(TX_MODE);
  c.pin_bck = PIN_BCLK; c.pin_ws = PIN_LRC; c.pin_data = PIN_DIN;
  c.sample_rate = 44100; c.channels = 2; c.bits_per_sample = 16;
  i2s.begin(c); gAudioCallMode = false;
}
void i2sCallMode() {
  i2s.end();
  auto c = i2s.defaultConfig(RXTX_MODE);
  c.pin_bck = PIN_BCLK; c.pin_ws = PIN_LRC; c.pin_data = PIN_DIN; c.pin_data_rx = PIN_MIC;
  c.sample_rate = CALL_RATE; c.channels = 2; c.bits_per_sample = 32;
  i2s.begin(c); gAudioCallMode = true;
}

// call audio task: speakers <- rxRing (upsampled x2/x4), mic -> txRing (decimated /2 or /4), half-duplex ducking
void callAudioTask(void *) {
  static int32_t frame[2 * 64];             // 64 stereo 32-bit frames = 2 ms @ 32 kHz
  float duckHold = 0;
  for (;;) {
    if (!gAudioCallMode) { vTaskDelay(pdMS_TO_TICKS(10)); continue; }
    const int up = gScoWide ? 2 : 4;        // 16k -> 32k, 8k -> 32k
    const int nIn = 64 / up;
    // speakers
    int16_t v[32] = {0}; size_t got = 0;
    uint8_t *d = (uint8_t *)xRingbufferReceiveUpTo(rxRing, &got, 0, nIn * 2);
    if (d) { memcpy(v, d, got); vRingbufferReturnItem(rxRing, d); }
    float energy = 0; const float g = (gCallVol + 1) / 16.0f;
    for (int i = 0; i < nIn; i++) {
      energy += fabsf(v[i]);
      int16_t a = v[i], b = (i + 1 < nIn) ? v[i + 1] : v[i];
      for (int k = 0; k < up; k++) { int32_t s = (int32_t)((a + (b - a) * k / up) * g) << 16; frame[2 * (i * up + k)] = s; frame[2 * (i * up + k) + 1] = s; }
    }
    i2s.write((uint8_t *)frame, sizeof(frame));
    if (energy / nIn > 600) duckHold = 0.15f; else duckHold = fmaxf(0, duckHold - 0.002f);
    // mic (left slot, SPH0645: 18-bit data MSB-aligned in 32 bits)
    size_t n = i2s.readBytes((uint8_t *)frame, sizeof(frame));
    int16_t out[32]; const int frames = n / 8, dn = up; int m = 0;
    const float mg = MIC_GAIN * (duckHold > 0 ? DUCK_GAIN : 1.0f);
    for (int i = 0; i + dn <= frames && m < 32; i += dn) {
      int32_t acc = 0; for (int k = 0; k < dn; k++) acc += frame[2 * (i + k)] >> 14;
      out[m++] = (int16_t)constrain((int32_t)(acc / dn * mg), -32768, 32767);
    }
    if (m) { xRingbufferSend(txRing, out, m * 2, 0); esp_hf_client_outgoing_data_ready(); }
  }
}

// find-my-glasses chime: fixed level, ignores media volume and mute; only outside calls
void playChimeBurst() {
  static int16_t buf[2 * 441];               // 10 ms @ 44.1 kHz stereo
  static uint32_t ph = 0;
  const uint32_t t = millis() % 1000;
  const float f = t < 150 ? 1568.0f : (t < 300 ? 2093.0f : 0);   // G6 - C7 blip, then silence
  for (int i = 0; i < 441; i++) { int16_t s = f ? (int16_t)(12000 * sinf(2 * PI * f * (ph++) / 44100.0f)) : 0; buf[2 * i] = buf[2 * i + 1] = s; }
  i2s.write((uint8_t *)buf, sizeof(buf));
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- battery
uint8_t batteryPercent() {
  // GPIO15 is ADC2. ADC2 conflicts with Wi-Fi (unused here); retry a few times anyway.
  uint32_t mv = 0; int ok = 0;
  for (int i = 0; i < 8; i++) { int r = analogReadMilliVolts(PIN_VBAT); if (r > 0) { mv += r; ok++; } }
  if (!ok) return 0;
  float v = 2.0f * mv / ok / 1000.0f;                 // 100k/100k divider
  static const float V[] = {3.30f, 3.60f, 3.70f, 3.75f, 3.80f, 3.90f, 4.00f, 4.10f, 4.20f};
  static const uint8_t P[] = {0, 10, 25, 40, 50, 65, 80, 92, 100};
  if (v <= V[0]) return 0;
  for (int i = 1; i < 9; i++) if (v < V[i]) return P[i - 1] + (uint8_t)((v - V[i - 1]) / (V[i] - V[i - 1]) * (P[i] - P[i - 1]));
  return 100;
}

void setup() {

  eqCfg.freq_low = 250; eqCfg.freq_high = 4000;

  gInvertSwipe = prefs.getBool("inv", false);

  a2dp.set_default_bt_mode(ESP_BT_MODE_BTDM);         // classic (A2DP) + BLE at the same time
  a2dp.set_on_connection_state_changed(onConnState);
  a2dp.set_on_audio_state_changed(onAudioState);
  a2dp.set_avrc_rn_playstatus_callback(onPlayStatus);

  a2dp.set_avrc_metadata_callback(onMetadata);
  a2dp.set_volume(gVolume);
  a2dp.start(DEVICE_NAME, true);                      // auto-reconnect to the last phone

  hfpBegin();                                         // HFP client on the same Bluedroid instance as A2DP
  bleBegin();

  xTaskCreatePinnedToCore(callAudioTask, "callAudio", 4096, nullptr, 10, nullptr, 1);
  uint8_t e = gEq; chEq->setValue(&e, 1);
  Serial.printf("Muse Glasses ready, free heap %u\n", ESP.getFreeHeap());
}

void loop() {
  pollTouch();

  if (gRecalibrate) { gRecalibrate = false; calibrate(); }
  if (gScoChanged) {                                  // SCO up/down: switch the I2S bus (phone suspends A2DP during calls)
    gScoChanged = false;
    if (gScoUp && !gAudioCallMode) { i2sCallMode(); Serial.printf("SCO %s, free heap %u\n", gScoWide ? "mSBC 16k" : "CVSD 8k", ESP.getFreeHeap()); }
    else if (!gScoUp && gAudioCallMode) { i2sMusicMode(); applyEq(gEq); }
  }
  if (gCallDirty) { gCallDirty = false; notifyU8(chCall, gCall); }
  if (gChimeUntil && !gAudioCallMode) {
    static bool paused = false;
    if (millis() < gChimeUntil) { if (gPlaying && !paused) { a2dp.pause(); paused = true; } playChimeBurst(); return; }
    gChimeUntil = 0; paused = false;
  }
  if (gVolDirty)  { gVolDirty = false;  notifyU8(chVol, gVolume); }
  if (gPlayDirty) { gPlayDirty = false; notifyU8(chPlay, gPlaying); }
  if (gLinkDirty) { gLinkDirty = false; notifyU8(chLink, gLink); }
  if (gTrackDirty) {
    gTrackDirty = false;
    char buf[181];
    portENTER_CRITICAL(&gMux);
    snprintf(buf, sizeof(buf), "%s\x1F%s", gTitle, gArtist);   // U+241F-style unit separator between title and artist
    portEXIT_CRITICAL(&gMux);
    chTrack->setValue((uint8_t *)buf, strlen(buf));
    if (bleClient) chTrack->notify();
  }

  static uint32_t tBatt = 0;
  if (millis() - tBatt > 5000 || tBatt == 0) {      // every 5 s
    tBatt = millis() | 1;
    uint8_t pct = batteryPercent();
    chBatt->setValue(&pct, 1);
    if (bleClient) chBatt->notify();
  }
  delay(10);
}

class GattIds {
  static final service = Guid('9f3c0001-2b6e-4c1a-8f5d-6a7e0b1c2d3e');

  static final callState = Guid('9f3c0009-2b6e-4c1a-8f5d-6a7e0b1c2d3e'); // uint8 0 idle 1 in 2 out 3 active  R/N
  static final batteryService = Guid('180f');
  static final batteryLevel = Guid('2a19');
}

class GlassesClient {
  static const deviceName = 'Muse Glasses';
  BluetoothDevice? _dev;
  final Map<Guid, BluetoothCharacteristic> _ch = {};
  final _subs = <StreamSubscription>[];

  final volume = StreamController<int>.broadcast();
  final playing = StreamController<bool>.broadcast();
  final track = StreamController<(String, String)>.broadcast();
  final eq = StreamController<int>.broadcast();
  final link = StreamController<int>.broadcast();
  final battery = StreamController<int>.broadcast();
  final connection = StreamController<bool>.broadcast();
  final call = StreamController<int>.broadcast();
  final rssi = StreamController<int>.broadcast();       // dBm, from readRssi() or advertising
  Timer? _rssiTimer;

  Stream<List<ScanResult>> scan({Duration timeout = const Duration(seconds: 8)}) {
    FlutterBluePlus.startScan(withServices: [GattIds.service], timeout: timeout);
    return FlutterBluePlus.scanResults;
  }

  Future<void> connect(BluetoothDevice d) async {
    await FlutterBluePlus.stopScan();
    _dev = d;
    await d.connect(autoConnect: false);
    _subs.add(d.connectionState.listen((s) => connection.add(s == BluetoothConnectionState.connected)));
    _rssiTimer?.cancel();
    _rssiTimer = Timer.periodic(const Duration(milliseconds: 500), (_) async {
      try { rssi.add(await d.readRssi()); } catch (_) {}
    });
    try {
      await d.requestMtu(185); // Android only; iOS negotiates on its own
    } catch (_) {}
    for (final svc in await d.discoverServices()) {
      for (final c in svc.characteristics) {
        _ch[c.uuid] = c;
      }
    }
    await _listenU8(GattIds.volume, volume);
    await _listenU8(GattIds.eq, eq);
    await _listenU8(GattIds.link, link);
    await _listenU8(GattIds.batteryLevel, battery);
    await _listenU8(GattIds.callState, call);
    await _listen(GattIds.play, (v) => playing.add(v.isNotEmpty && v[0] == 1));
    await _listen(GattIds.track, (v) {
      final parts = utf8.decode(v, allowMalformed: true).split('\u001F');
      track.add((parts.first, parts.length > 1 ? parts[1] : ''));
    });
  }

  Future<void> _listenU8(Guid id, StreamController<int> out) =>
      _listen(id, (v) { if (v.isNotEmpty) out.add(v[0]); });

  Future<void> _listen(Guid id, void Function(List<int>) onValue) async {
    final c = _ch[id];
    if (c == null) return;
    _subs.add(c.onValueReceived.listen(onValue));
    if (c.properties.notify) await c.setNotifyValue(true);
    if (c.properties.read) onValue(await c.read());
  }

  Future<void> _write(Guid id, int v) async => _ch[id]?.write([v & 0xFF], withoutResponse: false);

  Future<void> setVolume(int v) => _write(GattIds.volume, v.clamp(0, 127));
  Future<void> setPlaying(bool p) => _write(GattIds.play, p ? 1 : 0);
  Future<void> setEq(int preset) => _write(GattIds.eq, preset.clamp(0, 3));
  /// Find my glasses: chime for [seconds] at a fixed level, even if the media volume is 0. 0 stops it.
  Future<void> playFindSound([int seconds = 10]) => _write(GattIds.findChime, seconds.clamp(0, 60));

  /// While NOT connected: scan for the service and report the advertising RSSI ("hotter / colder").
  StreamSubscription<List<ScanResult>> watchAdvertisedRssi() {
    FlutterBluePlus.startScan(withServices: [GattIds.service], continuousUpdates: true, removeIfGone: const Duration(seconds: 6));
    return FlutterBluePlus.scanResults.listen((rs) {
      for (final r in rs) {
        rssi.add(r.rssi);
      }
    });
  }

  Future<void> setConfig({bool invertSwipe = false, bool recalibrate = false}) =>
      _write(GattIds.config, (invertSwipe ? 1 : 0) | (recalibrate ? 2 : 0));

  Future<void> disconnect() async {
    _rssiTimer?.cancel();
    for (final s in _subs) {
      await s.cancel();
    }
    _subs.clear();
    _ch.clear();
    await _dev?.disconnect();
  }
}

const eqNames = ['Flat', 'Bass+', 'Vocal', 'Treble'];
const linkNames = ['Not connected', 'Connected', 'Streaming'];
const callNames = ['No call', 'Incoming call: tap to answer, hold to reject', 'Calling…', 'In a call: tap to hang up'];

class LastSeen {
  LastSeen(this.when, this.lat, this.lon);
  final DateTime when;
  final double? lat, lon;
}

class GlassesState extends ChangeNotifier {
  GlassesState(this.client) {
    _subs.addAll([
      client.connection.stream.listen((c) {
        if (connected && !c) _rememberDisconnect(); // link just dropped: save time + phone location
        connected = c;
        if (c) lastRssiAt = DateTime.now();
        notifyListeners();
      }),
      client.call.stream.listen((v) { callState = v; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.rssi.stream.listen((r) {
        rssi = rssi == null ? r.toDouble() : rssi! * 0.7 + r * 0.3; // smooth the ±5–10 dB jitter
        lastRssiAt = DateTime.now();
        notifyListeners();
      }),
      client.volume.stream.listen((v) { volume = v; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.playing.stream.listen((p) { playing = p; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.track.stream.listen((t) { title = t.$1; artist = t.$2; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.eq.stream.listen((e) { eq = e; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.link.stream.listen((l) { audioLink = l; notifyListeners(); }),
      client.battery.stream.listen((b) { battery = b; notifyListeners(); }),
    ]);
    _loadLastSeen();
  }

  final GlassesClient client;
  final _subs = <StreamSubscription>[];

  bool connected = false;
  int volume = 64, eq = 0, audioLink = 0, battery = 0;
  bool playing = false, invertSwipe = false;
  String title = '', artist = '';
  int callState = 0;
  double? rssi;          // smoothed dBm
  DateTime? lastRssiAt;  // last time we heard the glasses at all
  LastSeen? lastSeen;    // phone location at the last disconnect

  bool get inRange => lastRssiAt != null && DateTime.now().difference(lastRssiAt!) < const Duration(seconds: 8);

  /// 0 = cold … 1 = hot. −95 dBm ≈ edge of range, −45 dBm ≈ right next to the phone.
  double get warmth => rssi == null ? 0 : ((rssi! + 95) / 50).clamp(0.0, 1.0);

  /// Very rough distance from a log-distance model (txPower −59 dBm @ 1 m, n = 2.5). Indoors it is only a hint.
  String get distanceHint {
    if (rssi == null || !inRange) return 'out of range';
    final d = math.pow(10, (-59 - rssi!) / 25).toDouble();
    if (d < 1) return 'within ~1 m';
    if (d < 3) return '≈ 1–3 m';
    if (d < 8) return '≈ 3–8 m';
    return '> 8 m';
  }

  Future<void> playFindSound() => client.playFindSound(10);
  Future<void> stopFindSound() => client.playFindSound(0);
  StreamSubscription<List<ScanResult>> watchWhileDisconnected() => client.watchAdvertisedRssi();

  Future<void> _rememberDisconnect() async {
    double? lat, lon;
    try {
      var perm = await Geolocator.checkPermission();
      if (perm == LocationPermission.denied) perm = await Geolocator.requestPermission();
      if (perm == LocationPermission.whileInUse || perm == LocationPermission.always) {
        final p = await Geolocator.getCurrentPosition(locationSettings: const LocationSettings(accuracy: LocationAccuracy.high, timeLimit: Duration(seconds: 10)));
        lat = p.latitude; lon = p.longitude;
      }
    } catch (_) {/* no fix: keep the time only */}
    lastSeen = LastSeen(DateTime.now(), lat, lon);
    final sp = await SharedPreferences.getInstance(); // stored on this phone only
    await sp.setString('lastSeen.when', lastSeen!.when.toIso8601String());
    if (lat != null && lon != null) { await sp.setDouble('lastSeen.lat', lat); await sp.setDouble('lastSeen.lon', lon); }
    else { await sp.remove('lastSeen.lat'); await sp.remove('lastSeen.lon'); }
    notifyListeners();
  }

  Future<void> _loadLastSeen() async {
    final sp = await SharedPreferences.getInstance();
    final w = sp.getString('lastSeen.when');
    if (w != null) { lastSeen = LastSeen(DateTime.parse(w), sp.getDouble('lastSeen.lat'), sp.getDouble('lastSeen.lon')); notifyListeners(); }
  }

  Stream<List<ScanResult>> scan() => client.scan();
  Future<void> connect(BluetoothDevice d) => client.connect(d);
  Future<void> disconnect() => client.disconnect();

  Future<void> setVolume(int v) { volume = v; notifyListeners(); return client.setVolume(v); }
  Future<void> togglePlay() => client.setPlaying(!playing);
  Future<void> setEq(int e) { eq = e; notifyListeners(); return client.setEq(e); }
  Future<void> setInvertSwipe(bool v) { invertSwipe = v; notifyListeners(); return client.setConfig(invertSwipe: v); }
  Future<void> recalibrate() => client.setConfig(invertSwipe: invertSwipe, recalibrate: true);

  @override
  void dispose() {
    for (final s in _subs) {
      s.cancel();
    }
    super.dispose();
  }
}

class _FindScreenState extends State<FindScreen> {

  @override
  void initState() {
    super.initState();
    final s = context.read<GlassesState>();
    if (!s.connected) _scan = s.watchWhileDisconnected(); // advertising RSSI while not connected

  @override
  void dispose() {
    if (_scan != null) { _scan!.cancel(); FlutterBluePlus.stopScan(); }
    super.dispose();

  String _ago(DateTime t) {
    final d = DateTime.now().difference(t);
    if (d.inMinutes < 1) return 'just now';
    if (d.inHours < 1) return '${d.inMinutes} min ago';

13 Find my glasses

Three tools in the app's Find screen, none of which need a server or an account:

Hotter / colderRSSI meterWhile connected the app polls readRssi() every 500 ms; when not, it scans for the service UUID and uses the advertising RSSI. Smoothed, then shown as cold → hot with a rough distance (BLE RSSI is ±5–10 dB noisy: treat it as a direction hint).
Last seenWhere you lost themOn every disconnect the app stores the time and the phone's location (geolocator) in shared_preferences, on the phone only. The glasses have no GPS, so this is where the phone was when the link dropped.
Play soundChime, even when mutedWrites N seconds to 9f3c0008. The firmware pauses music and writes a G6–C7 blip straight to I²S at a fixed level, bypassing the A2DP volume. "Stop" writes 0.
Limits: the glasses must be switched on and within BLE range (~10–30 m indoors). If the battery is flat or the switch is off there's nothing to hear and only "last seen" helps. On iOS, background BLE scanning is throttled and needs the bluetooth-central background mode; the disconnect location needs "While Using" (or "Always") location permission, so the app saves it when it next runs if the OS suspended it.

14 Build order (bench first)

  1. Breadboard audio. QT Py + one amp + speaker, pair a phone and play. Add the second amp with the 330 kΩ and confirm stereo.
  2. AVRCP. Check play/pause, volume sync and metadata on both iPhone and Android.
  3. Touch. Copper pads on a printed pod blank: log raw values, tune thresholds.
  4. Calls. Add the SPH0645 on the breadboard (GPIO22), place a call from the phone: check answer / hang up / reject, both directions of audio, mSBC vs CVSD, and the free heap. Then music → call → music transitions.
  5. BLE. Test the GATT with nRF Connect while streaming; watch free heap.
  6. App. Connect + Now Playing first, then call state, Find (RSSI, last seen, Play sound), EQ and Settings.
  7. Power. Cell, charger, switch; measure streaming current and run a full play-time test.
  8. Frame integration. The 14 animated steps of this guide.

15 Risks & caveats

  • Fit: the QT Py (22 × 18 mm) and amp breakouts (19 × 18 mm) don't fit thin wire temples, so v1 accepts clip-on hinge and ear-tip pods.
  • Battery life: ~1 h of music or ~45–60 min of calls on 120 mAh (estimates); ~3× with a 350 mAh cell in a longer left pod.
  • Call quality: the mic is ~10 cm from the mouth (behind the ear, for looks): lower voice level and more room noise than earbuds. Tune MIC_GAIN; keep the phone's noise reduction on.
  • Echo: open-ear speakers leak into the mic. Mitigated by the phone's AEC + firmware half-duplex ducking + moderate call volume; expect some double-talk clipping.
  • iOS: HFP calls work, and iOS sends calls to the glasses when they're the selected audio route (it normally picks the last connected HFP device). iOS won't show the Classic link to apps, so the app relies on the GATT call state. Background BLE scanning is throttled, and the last-seen location depends on the location permission. Android behaves more predictably.
  • Legacy HFP data API: uses ESP-IDF's internal codec + legacy PCM callbacks; a future core may drop them (then: external-codec callbacks + own CVSD/mSBC).
  • Hinge wiring fatigues: pin the hinges or leave silicone loops.
  • Open-ear sound: these speakers resonate near 950 Hz — thin bass, some leakage. Keep gain low.
  • Touch through paint: non-conductive finish only; isolate the pads from the metal wire temple (Kapton under the pod channel).
  • Stack stability: A2DP + BLE is RAM-tight; Wi-Fi is off the table.
  • LiPo near the head: protected cell, 100 mA charge, no charging while worn.
  • 9 vs 8 cross-frame wires: see the harness section.
